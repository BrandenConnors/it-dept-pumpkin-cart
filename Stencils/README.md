# Pumpkin cutting stencils

Open `Pumpkin_Cutting_Stencils.pdf` and print at Actual Size / 100% on US Letter paper. Do not fit, shrink, or auto-scale. Confirm the ruler measures exactly 100 mm.

| Page | Piece | Quantity |
|---|---|---:|
| 2 | Bed side panels | 2 |
| 3 | Rear bed panel | 1 |
| 4 | Roof skin | 1 |
| 5 | Cab skirts | 2 mirrored |
| 6 | Rear wheel arches | 2 mirrored |
| 7 | Front fender contact footprints | 2 |
| 8 | HF2 hood fitting reference | 1 hood, or combined hood/fender shell |

Crosses are spike contact marks on the flesh face, not drilled holes. Rear-panel width varies with bed-side flesh thickness. Keep the inner flesh thick enough for the 6 mm spikes. Paper-check all patterns against the cart; moving the adjustable fender pads changes the contact spacing for a one-piece shell.

Page 8 shows the carrier/tower envelope and spike contacts at true scale, not a fixed outer pumpkin silhouette or cutting depth. Hollow and adjust progressively. Original 30 mm hood-cap and 49 x 61 mm cavity patterns are superseded.
