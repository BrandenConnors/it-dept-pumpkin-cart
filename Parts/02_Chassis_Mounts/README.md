## Fit and printing notes

- **16A_front_end_adapter_TRIAL.stl:** Front adapter omitted in Branden's assembled configuration. Retained as an alternate mount option.
- **16B_rear_end_adapter_TRIAL.stl:** Retain your successful spacer stack and mounting configuration. 8 mm trial adapter; rear chassis slots nominal 86 mm centers. Fit template 00D first. Chassis screw thread/length not yet confirmed.
- **17_mount_shim_12mm_print_4.stl:** Retain your successful spacer stack and mounting configuration. 12 mm provisional spacer between adapter and floor underside. Adjust height only after checking clearances under load.
- **17B_mount_shim_2mm_OPTIONAL.stl:** Retain your successful spacer stack and mounting configuration. Optional 2 mm adjustment washer. Print only as required; use equal stacks left/right. Longer bolts may be needed.
