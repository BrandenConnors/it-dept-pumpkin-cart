Use the master [build guide](../../Documentation/Build_Guide.pdf). STL files use millimeters and retain their supplied print orientations. One STL is one piece; print the quantities below.

| 19_IT_DEPT_plaque_print_2.stl | 2 | Required |

## Fit and printing notes

- **18_5mm_LED_bezel_print_2.stl:** Optional: thread through flange ears to retain bezel in rind. Actual LED fit/retention must be checked; wiring not supplied.
- **19_IT_DEPT_plaque_print_2.stl:** Paint raised letters white. Two narrow ties/thread loops per plaque pass through paired ear/bed holes, with rind between. Do not cinch against wet rind.
- **20A_front_bumper.stl:** M3x10 into front floor inserts; original footprint retained.
- **20B_rear_bumper_adjustable.stl:** Two M3x20 bolts with washers through arms and floor slots into 20C blocks. Nominal travel 16.6 mm; fit on actual chassis.
- **20C_rear_bumper_insert_block_print_2.stl:** Install insert in top face. Block rests below rear reinforced slot at Z=-8; hold while starting bolt, slide bumper then snug.
