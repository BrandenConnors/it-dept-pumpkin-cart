## Fit and printing notes

- **01_switch_24port_body.stl:** Open top UP; 0.16 mm layers; no supports expected. Retain lid with two narrow removable bands after painting port recesses.
- **01_switch_24port_lid.stl:** Flat plain face DOWN; engraved vents UP. Center flat lid on rim and retain with two narrow bands; no snap fit.
- **02_switch_8port_body.stl:** Open top UP; 0.16 mm layers; no supports expected. Retain lid with two narrow removable bands after painting port recesses.
- **02_switch_8port_lid.stl:** Flat plain face DOWN; engraved vents UP. Center flat lid on rim and retain with two narrow bands; no snap fit.
- **03_bullet_camera.stl:** Open underside DOWN; 0.16 mm layers. Inspect 10 mm inner roof bridge; use internal supports if bridging is poor. Paint recessed lens black.
- **04_dome_camera.stl:** Base ring DOWN; 0.16 mm layers. Hollow underside; inspect top bridge. Paint upper dome smoky gray and lens black.
- **05_toolbox_body.stl:** Open top UP; no supports expected. Retain lid with two narrow bands. Paint latches silver.
- **05_toolbox_lid.stl:** Flat underside DOWN; handle UP. The handle bridges about 9 mm. Inspect bridge; treat handle as decorative, not a lifting point.
- **06_cable_coil.stl:** Flat backing DOWN; 0.16 mm layers, no supports expected. Paint retaining straps black. Decorative rigid coil, not flexible cable.
- **07_pc_tower_body.stl:** Open top UP; no supports expected. Print only if desired after the load check. Paint front slots / power button.
- **07_pc_tower_lid.stl:** Flat face DOWN. Center on body rim and retain with two narrow bands; no snap fit.
