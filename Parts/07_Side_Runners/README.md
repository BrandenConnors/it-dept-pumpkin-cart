## Fit and printing notes

- **08_offset_spacer_5mm_optional.stl:** Offset spacers: two of equal thickness per panel; choose 5 OR 10 mm.
- **09_offset_spacer_10mm_optional.stl:** Offset spacers: two of equal thickness per panel; choose 5 OR 10 mm.

Cab upper clamps and forward bed hangers need support under overhangs. Backings print flat with spikes up. Four 16 mm bed spacers are in `05_Bed`. No additional floor holes are needed.
