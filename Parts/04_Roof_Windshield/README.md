## Fit and printing notes

- **09_rear_roof_post_print_2.stl:** Print on side; supports may be needed under wider foot/ears. Pegs locate; two M3x8 bolts fix foot; one M3x16 fixes roof clevis. Front ears accept inserts from rear side.
- **10_front_roof_post_print_2.stl:** Print on side; supports may be needed under wider foot/ears. Pegs locate; two M3x8 bolts fix foot; one M3x16 fixes roof clevis. Front ears accept inserts from rear side.
- **21_windshield_plate_print_4.stl:** Flat face DOWN. Four M3x10 bolts clamp thin clear sheet to inward holes on front post ears. Sheet nominal 0.75 mm; adjust hardware to actual thickness.

Use R2 spiked roof, crown up with clevis feet down; inspect supports under the crown and remove gently. Windshield is separately cut clear sheet (nominal 0.75 mm), not a printed STL.
